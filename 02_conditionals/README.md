# Conditionals

Exercises to practice conditional control statements (if, else if, else, switch).

## Exercises

- **[1. Adult Check:](./01_adult_check.c)** Determines if a person is an adult based on their age (18 or older).
- **[2. Max of Three Numbers:](./02_max_of_three.c)** Finds the maximum value among three input integers.
- **[3. Leap Year:](./03_leap_year.c)** Determines if a year is a leap year (divisible by 4 and not by 100, unless divisible by 400).
- **[4. Even, Odd, or Zero:](./04_even_odd.c)** Determines if an integer is even, odd, or zero.
- **[5. Grade Letter:](./05_grade_letter.c)** Converts a numerical grade (0-100) to a letter (A, B, C, D, F).
- **[6. Basic Calculator:](./06_calculator.c)** Performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division between two numbers.
- **[7. Triangle Classification:](./07_triangle_type.c)** Checks if three lengths form a valid triangle and classifies it (Equilateral, Isosceles, Scalene).
- **[8. Zodiac Sign:](./08_zodiac_sign.c)** Displays the zodiac sign based on the day and month of birth.
- **[9. Parking Fee:](./09_parking_fee.c)** Calculates the parking fee based on the hours parked using tiered rates.
- **[10. Roman Numerals:](./10_roman_numerals.c)** Converts an integer between 1 and 10 to its Roman numeral equivalent.
